FAQs

Google My Business — Your Questions Answered

What is Google My Business (GMB)?

Google My Business (now called Google Business Profile) is a free tool from Google that lets you manage how your business appears in Google Search and Maps — including your business name, location, hours, reviews, photos, and more.

How long does GMB optimization take to show results?

Most businesses start seeing improved visibility and increased customer interactions within 2–4 weeks. Significant ranking improvements in the Local Pack typically occur within 1–3 months of consistent optimization.

Do you handle Google reviews and reputation management?

Yes. We implement review generation strategies, respond professionally to all reviews (positive and negative), and help you build a stellar online reputation that attracts more customers.

Can you manage multiple business locations?

Absolutely. We have experience managing GMB profiles for multi-location businesses, ensuring consistent optimization, posting, and review management across all your locations.

Is GMB optimization a one-time service?

While initial setup and optimization is a one-time process, ongoing management (posting, review responses, performance tracking) is recommended for sustained results and continued growth in local visibility.
