FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions About Web Development

How long does it take to build a website?

A standard business website takes 2–4 weeks. E-commerce or custom web applications may take 4–8 weeks depending on complexity, integrations, and feature requirements.

Will my website be mobile-friendly?

Absolutely. Every website we build uses a mobile-first development approach, ensuring flawless performance and user experience across smartphones, tablets, and desktops.

Do you provide hosting and domain services?

Yes. We can help you choose and set up reliable hosting and domain registration. We recommend premium hosting solutions for optimal speed and security.

Can I update the website content myself?

Yes. We build websites with user-friendly CMS (Content Management Systems) like WordPress, so you can easily update text, images, and content without any coding knowledge.

Do you offer post-launch maintenance?

Yes. We offer flexible maintenance packages including security updates, performance monitoring, content updates, and technical support to keep your site running smoothly.
