FAQs

Common Questions About Graphic Design

How many design concepts will I receive?

For logo and branding projects, we typically provide 3–5 initial concepts. For other design projects, we present 2–3 directions for you to choose from and refine.

What file formats do you deliver?

We deliver all standard formats including AI, PSD, PDF, SVG, PNG, and JPG. For print projects, we provide CMYK press-ready files with proper bleed and crop marks.

How long does a typical design project take?

Logo design: 5–7 business days. Brand identity: 10–15 days. Social media templates: 3–5 days. Marketing materials: 4–7 days. Rush deliveries are available upon request.

Do you offer ongoing design support?

Yes! We offer monthly retainer packages for businesses needing regular design work — social media creatives, marketing materials, and brand asset management.

Can you redesign my existing brand?

Absolutely. We specialize in brand refreshes and complete redesigns — evolving your visual identity while preserving brand equity your audience already knows.
